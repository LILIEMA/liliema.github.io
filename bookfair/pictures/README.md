# Pictures / Images

Illustrations: Unni Pohjonen for LILIEMA, 2026. Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The files in this folder are web copies adapted from the original drawings: trimmed and centred on a square, resized to 600 × 600 pixels, converted to greyscale, and with faint lines slightly darkened; on ear.png a stray pen stroke was removed. In the second batch (October 2026), small specks from the scan were removed, and the lines of man.png, woman.png, millet.png and tree.png were made slightly stronger.

rain.png is a stand-in until the illustrator's own drawing: Unni Pohjonen's cloud drawing with raindrops added by LILIEMA (drawn with Claude, an AI by Anthropic).

When reusing a picture, please credit it as follows: Illustration: Unni Pohjonen for LILIEMA, CC BY 4.0. For rain.png: Illustration: Unni Pohjonen for LILIEMA, adapted (raindrops added), CC BY 4.0.
